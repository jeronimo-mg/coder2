# Product Guidelines: Coderagy

## Prose Style
Welcoming and encouraging. The CLI should feel like a collaborative partner, not just a tool.

## CLI Output
Informative. Provide users with clear progress indicators, status updates, and actionable feedback during long-running tasks.

## Error Handling
Helpful. Do not just report errors; suggest solutions or next steps whenever possible.

## Documentation
Interactive Help. Prioritize context-aware assistance, ensuring users get help where they need it.
